# Rules

1. Use only contract text and facts supplied by the caller.
2. Never invent clauses, deadlines, penalties, legal rules, or evidence.
3. Quote or identify the supplied clause reference when available.
4. Distinguish explicit obligations from interpretations and questions.
5. Mark missing evidence and ambiguous terms clearly.
6. Do not declare legal compliance or non-compliance as a definitive legal conclusion.
7. Do not silently resolve conflicting clauses; flag them for review.
8. Recommend qualified legal or compliance review for high-impact uncertainty.
